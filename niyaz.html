About me

I started out in mechanical engineering, then moved into software because I liked the faster feedback loop between building something and watching people actually use it. That switch means I've had to be deliberate about learning — I don't take the fundamentals for granted, and I still enjoy the process of picking a new tool or pattern apart until I understand it.

Day to day, I work across the full stack: React and Redux on the front end, Node.js and Express on the back end, MongoDB underneath. I've owned features end to end — from the first API route to the interface a user actually clicks through — and I'd rather ship something small and working than something large and half-finished.

Most of my recent work has been on an e-learning platform that turns PDFs into interactive digital books, and a real-time attendance and leave management system. Both are live projects, not exercises, which keeps me honest about writing code other people can actually maintain.
